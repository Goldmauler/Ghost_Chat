# Rooms

A **room** is a private channel for you and your team.

- **Create Room** makes a new one and gives you an **invite**.
- **Join with Invite** lets you paste an invite someone sent you.

Everything you send is encrypted on your machine with AES-256-GCM before it leaves. The server only ever stores scrambled text. No account and no setup.

Rooms delete themselves after 24 hours with no messages. You can change that with the `rezo.roomExpiry` setting.
